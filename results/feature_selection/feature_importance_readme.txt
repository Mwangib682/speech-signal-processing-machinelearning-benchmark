Feature importance report\n=========================\nANOVA, Chi-square, Mutual Information and selection frequency are calculated from the TRAINING data only.\nChi-square is calculated on min-max-scaled continuous features; interpret it as a nonnegative feature ranking heuristic, not a classical contingency-table independence test.\nClass-specific effect sizes are the largest one-versus-rest standardized mean differences among each class's top 10; missing values do not imply zero effect.\nPermutation importance is calculated on the HELD-OUT TEST set for descriptive interpretation of the final selected classifier. Do not use test importance to choose or tune features.\nConsensus score = mean of percentile ranks (0-100) for ANOVA F, Chi-square, Mutual Information and seven-method selection frequency. It is a descriptive research-defined composite, not a probability.\nBecause all methods can select correlated features, selection frequency and permutation importance should be interpreted cautiously.\n
